      Sources, methodology & important information

      Primary source: The financial and activity information displayed here is compiled from Iowa counties’ 2025 Opioid Funds – Public Annual Report submissions. The form is used by participating local governments under the Iowa Opioid Allocation Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). Publicly accessible copies of reports identified for this project can be found through the DocumentCloud search for OSF-IA-2025. The Iowa Attorney General also provides the official opioid settlement information and Public Annual Report form.

      How the information was transcribed: Tesseract OCR (optical character recognition) was used to extract text from the source PDF reports. Activity descriptions and related narrative text were then copied into Microsoft Word and reviewed to identify and correct apparent OCR or transcription errors. The extracted and reviewed information was organized for display on this website. OCR and manual proofreading can still miss errors, particularly where source PDFs are scanned, unclear, or have complex formatting; the original county report should be consulted when exact wording or figures matter.

      How to read the figures: The Public Annual Report instructs local governments to complete its financial summary on a cash basis. It requests beginning balances, funds received, expenditures, and ending balances for the state fiscal-year reporting period. Its activity section requests the MOU remediation-use category, a description of each funded activity, and reported progress or outcomes. This website presents selected information transcribed and organized from those submissions; it is not the original report form.

      Coverage: This dataset presents county-reported information for state fiscal year 2025. It does not represent all opioid settlement funds distributed or spent in Iowa. In particular, it does not include separate city-level or state-level funds. City names in the search are geographic lookup aids: a city result takes you to the associated county’s figures and does not indicate that the city received or spent the amount displayed.

      Limitations: Figures and activity descriptions are attributed to the reporting local governments and are shown as reported, subject to OCR, manual transcription review, formatting, and categorization needed to compile the dataset. Although activity text was reviewed in Microsoft Word for apparent transcription errors, this process is not an independent verification or audit and cannot guarantee that every OCR or transcription error was identified. A blank or unavailable value means information was not reported or could not be determined from the source; it does not mean zero. This website does not independently audit, verify, or certify the reports, underlying transactions, eligibility or effectiveness of expenditures, or compliance with the MOU. Reported outcomes are those described by the reporting entity and are not independently evaluated. Statewide totals may exclude counties with missing values; reporting coverage should be considered when interpreting them.

      Independent project; no government affiliation: This website is an independent informational project. It is not operated, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by the State of Iowa, the Iowa Attorney General, any county or city, or any settlement administrator. Government names and documents are referenced only to identify source material. If information here differs from an original submission or official government record, consult the original source or responsible local government.

      No legal, accounting, or audit advice: This website is provided for general public information and transparency. It is not legal, accounting, financial, or audit advice, and it should not be relied upon to determine whether a particular expenditure is allowable or whether a local government has fulfilled its reporting obligations. For authoritative information, consult the applicable MOU, original report, or responsible government office.

      Corrections and questions: Please compare a questioned figure with the underlying report before drawing conclusions. Questions about a county’s submission should be directed to that county. Questions about Iowa’s reporting form or MOU may be directed to the Iowa Attorney General’s opioid settlement information page.
